Where the 20618 Sleeve protects your installations

NFPA-rated, reclosable flexible PVC hose sleeve. Built for industrial sites and job sites where abrasion, chemicals, and mechanical stress put your hoses to the test.

Urethane pumps & liquid membrane

Waterproofing and spray insulation crews rely on high-pressure hoses exposed to urethane, solvents, and abrasives. The 20618 sleeve wraps hose-and-fitting assemblies with no exterior seam, reducing premature wear and production downtime.

  • Protection for fittings and flex zones
  • Reopen for inspection without disassembly
  • Resistance to chemical splash

Construction sites

On sites where concrete, dust, and frequent movement damage hydraulic and pneumatic lines, our scuffguard provides a durable physical barrier. Fast installation even on hoses already in place.

  • Extreme, dusty environments
  • 50 ft effective length per unit
  • Wider section to cover fittings

General industrial protection

Plants, mines, forestry, transport: wherever hose and wire bundles need protection from abrasion, impact, and exposure. Custom solutions for large volumes and specific configurations.

  • NFPA compliance for regulated applications
  • Flexible PVC, no exterior seam
  • B2B orders and volume pricing

Safety & NFPA compliance

When your procedures require fire safety compliant equipment, the 20618 Sleeve meets NFPA requirements while making preventive maintenance easier thanks to its reclosable design.

  • NFPA rated
  • Upgrade without full replacement
  • Made in Canada

Why choose a reclosable sleeve?

Unlike rigid sleeves or sewn solutions, the 20618 Sleeve slides onto hoses already in place. One wider end covers fittings and the adjacent sleeve: zero friction points, zero exterior seam.

No hose disassembly required
