PROUDLY DESIGNED AND MANUFACTURED IN CANADA, the innovative, robust and high-performance protective sleeve (scuffguard) from 2ME PROTECTION INC. was created to protect hoses, cables and wires in the best possible way. It allows easier and faster installation on existing assemblies, lasts longer and adapts without compromise.

FITTED WITH A HOOK-AND-LOOP CLOSURE

  • Reopenable, reclosable and reusable;
  • Provides a perfect seal;
  • Makes it possible to inspect hoses, cables and wires and to upgrade them without complete disassembly and without replacement, so that production is not reduced.

NO EXTERIOR SEAM

  • No point of friction;
  • Protects fittings and flex zones.

IN VERY TOUGH, WATERPROOF FLEXIBLE PVC

  • Provides a high level of protection against solvents, abrasives, chemicals and impact
  • Highly resistant to alkalinity (Cement, fresh concrete or certain industrial products);
  • Keeps your equipment working steadily;
  • Reduces premature wear and extends the service life of the equipment.

COMPLIANT WITH NFPA STANDARDS

  • Meets strict NFPA fire prevention and safety codes.

WITH A JUNCTION SECTION

One of the two ends has a wider section specially designed to cover two hoses end to end in a continuous, even and secure way, while protecting the fittings and the next protective sleeve.

THE PROTECTIVE SLEEVE FROM 2ME PROTECTION INC. PROTECTS YOUR INSTALLATIONS IN THE BEST POSSIBLE WAY, IN EVERY SITUATION AND IN THE MOST HOSTILE ENVIRONMENTS.
